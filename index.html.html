    India's own record

    India Meteorological Department (IMD) measures national land temperature against the 1991–2020 average, a different baseline from the global series above. Do not compare the numbers directly.

      2025 was India's eighth-warmest year, 0.28°C above the 1991–2020 average. The decade 2016–2025 was the warmest on record at +0.32°C.

      January–February 2025 was the warmest winter in 124 years (+1.17°C), and the first heatwave of the year hit Goa and Maharashtra on 25 February.

      Ten of India's fifteen warmest years since 1901 fall between 2011 and 2025.

      How to read this: a temperature anomaly is the difference from a reference average. NASA GISTEMP uses 1951–1980. NOAA's Climate at a Glance series uses the 20th-century average. Colors are scaled to each record's own average, so the same year can look slightly different between datasets. The agencies use different methods, which is why rankings can differ by a place or two.

      Data snapshot: annual values 1880–2025 as of October 2026. The partial year 2026 is left out. India figures are as published by IMD; the 2009, 2010, 2016 and 2017 values come from IMD's January 2025 statement, and later statements may restate them.
